How a scene goes.

  1. You get a role.

    You are at the bakery. You want bread, and the baker asks if it should be sliced. Every scene is a moment you will meet in Norway, from the checkout to the parents' meeting.

  2. You say it. Out loud.

    Your line is on the screen with its meaning in your language. Listen first if you like, then say it like you would there. No typing, no word puzzles.

  3. Your phone gives you stars.★★★

    A speech model on your phone gives up to three stars and points at the sounds that matter, like takk and tak. Your voice never leaves the phone.

  4. Norway answers back.

    The other person replies, sometimes in their own dialect, and the scene moves on. Tap a reply to see it written and translated.
