How it works

1 · LISTENA real situation

The cashier asks about a bag, the bus driver about your stop, the neighbour about the dugnad. Real Norwegian voices, local dialects included.

2 · SPEAKSay your line out loud

No typing, no tapping words in order. You answer like you would at the till tomorrow morning.

3 · STARSScored on your phone

Your pronunciation is checked on the device, word by word. Tricky sounds like takk/tak and ny/ni get a tip. Your voice never leaves the phone.
