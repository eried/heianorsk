Privacy

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

No account

Heianorsk has no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Your voice stays on your phone

When you speak, the app records audio only to score your pronunciation. The scoring runs on your phone. Recordings are not uploaded and are deleted when you leave the scene.

What the app stores

Your settings (language, town, situations, reminders) and your progress (scenes played, stars, streak) are stored on your phone. On Android, this progress file can be included in your own Google backup, so it comes back on a new phone. You can export it as a file and delete it with "Start over" in Settings.

What the app downloads

On first use, the app downloads the pronunciation model, and later the scene packs you unlock, from api.heianorsk.ried.no. These downloads contain no personal data. Our server sees the request like any web server does (IP address, time) and keeps no profile of you.

Anonymous usage counts

To find out which stories and lines are too hard, the app sends small anonymous counts: that it was opened today, that it was installed, and when you finish a story, which story it was and how many stars each line got. Each count carries the app language, your level, the phone system (Android or iOS) and the app version. It carries no account, no device or install id, no town or country, and no audio, so we cannot tell whose counts they are or link two counts together. You can turn this off at any time in Profile, under "Share anonymous usage".

The app also checks our server for new or corrected stories and downloads them, in the same way as the other downloads.

Problem reports

If you tap the flag inside a story, you can send us a report: what kind of problem it is, a short note if you write one, the story and line, and a screenshot of the story screen unless you switch it off. Like the counts, it has no account or device id and no audio. Only we can see reports, and we use them to fix the story.

Purchases

Unlocking the full app is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We receive a confirmation of the purchase, not your payment details.

Children

Heianorsk is made for adults learning Norwegian. Some scenes include alcohol, dating or nudity at a sauna; you can hide those in Settings.
